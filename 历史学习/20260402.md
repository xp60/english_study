__语法：一般疑问句__

    This is my ...
    Is this your ...
    This is my handbag?
    is 提前到句首。
    Is this your handbag?
    如何判断是否为一般疑问句：如果回答yes or no可以回答就是一般疑问句。

__句子：__

    Excuse me!
    Yes?
    Is this your handbag?
    Pardon?
    Is this your handbag?
    Yes, it is.
    Thank you very much.
    Excuse me, is this your car?
    非标准句子：
    This your house?
