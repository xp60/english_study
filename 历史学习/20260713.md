    1. 特殊疑问句和一般疑问句？

__单词：__

|单词|词性|中文| 
|---|---|---|

|keyboard|||

__语法：__

    特殊疑问词 What:
        特殊疑问句 = 特殊疑问词 + 一般疑问句（去掉答案）
        My name is Leo.
        Is your name Leo?
        What is your name?
        Her name is Monica.
        Is her name Monica?
    
    特殊疑问词 Where:
        She is from Italy.
        Is she from Italy?
        Where is she from?
