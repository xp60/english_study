### 2026.06.01 一

__问题：__

    1. 询问某件物品是某人的？
    2. Yes 还能表示“干什么”？

__单词：__

|单词|词性|中文| 
|---|---|---|
|house||房子|
|passport||护照|

__语法：__

    一般疑问句： 
        一个单词提到句首。
        Is this your handbag?
        Is this your ...

        如何判断是否一般疑问句：是否可以用Yes or No回答，就是一般疑问句。

    肯定句：This is my handbag.
        This is my ...

__句子：__

    Excuse me!
    Yes? (声调上扬)
    Is this your handbag?
    Excuse me, is this your car?
    Yeah.
    This is my car.
    This is my house.
    This is my passport.
    This your house? (非标准语法)
    Pardon?
    Is this your handbag?
    Yes, it is.
    Thank you very much.
