### 2026.08.21 星期五

__问题：__

    1. 如何问某件物品是谁的？
    2. 如何表达“大概”或“可能”？
    3. 如何表达“这是Leo的笔”？

__单词：__

|单词|词性|中文| 
|---|---|---|
|blue|adj.|蓝色的|
|keyboard|n.|键盘|
|perhaps|adv.|可能|
|white|adj.|白色|
|catch|v.|接住|
|whose|adj.|谁的|
|watch|n.|手表|
|ticket|n.|票|
|shirt|n.|衬衫|
|is||的/是|
|coat|n.|外套|


__语法：__

    特殊疑问句：
        whose
        特殊疑问句 = 特殊疑问词 + 一般疑问句（去掉答案）
        This is her dress.
        Is this her dress?
        Whose is this dress? 不常见
        Whose dress is this? 常见
        Whose ___ is this?

    n. + 's
        含义--谁的
        This is Leo's pen.

__短语&句子：__

    My keyboard (am/are/is) bule.
    My keyboard is bule.
    Is that your car?
    I don't know. Perhaps it is.
    Her coat is white
    Is this shirt Tim's?  这个衬衫是Tim的吗？
    Here you are. Catch!
